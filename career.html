Careers & Faculty Opportunities

Be part of an established educational institution since 1944. Mentor motivated students, collaborate with caring colleagues, and grow professionally.

Work With Us

Educating character, inspiring intellect.

Dnyandeep English Medium School welcomes passionate educators who view teaching as a noble calling. We foster a disciplined yet warm academic atmosphere where teachers receive administrative support, respectful parent partnerships, and freedom to innovate within the curriculum.

Open Positions (2026–27)

  • Pre-Primary Educators: ECCEd / Montessori trained teachers with warmth and patience.
  • Primary Teachers (PRT): D.Ed / B.Ed in English, Mathematics, Marathi, or Hindi.
  • Secondary SSC Teachers (TGT): B.Sc/M.Sc + B.Ed in Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Mathematics.
  • Activity Instructors: Physical Education (B.P.Ed), Computer Lab Instructor, Art & Craft.
