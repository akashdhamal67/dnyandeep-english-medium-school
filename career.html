Work With Us

Shaping Character and Nurturing Knowledge

Dnyandeep English Medium School welcomes passionate, qualified, and student-focused educators who view teaching as a transformative responsibility. We foster a respectful, disciplined academic environment with strong administrative backing.

Current Faculty Requirements (2026–27)

  • Pre-Primary Teachers: ECCEd / Montessori trained educators with warmth and patience.
  • Primary Teachers (PRT): D.Ed / B.Ed for English, Mathematics, Environmental Studies, Marathi.
  • Secondary SSC Teachers (TGT): B.Sc/M.Sc + B.Ed in Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Mathematics, Social Sciences.
  • Activity Instructors: Physical Education (B.P.Ed), Computer Lab Instructor, Art & Craft.
