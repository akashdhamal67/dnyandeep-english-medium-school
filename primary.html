Primary Section (Std 1–4)

Building strong fundamentals in language, mathematics, and environmental awareness.

Class Range: Std 1 to Std 4

Strong Academic Foundations

Our Primary Section focuses on strong fundamentals in language, maths, and environmental awareness. The syllabus is structured in alignment with the Maharashtra State Board, ensuring structured progression, conceptual clarity, and positive study habits.

Learning Approach

We employ a concept-based, student-centric approach combining classroom learning, practical activities, and creative methods. Individual attention, regular assessments, doubt-clearing sessions, and revision programs ensure no child is left behind.

All-Round Student Development

Balance of academics, sports, arts, and practical learning. Students participate in regular sports periods, art and drawing, basic computer lab sessions, and cultural celebrations.
