Academics / Primary Section

Primary Section: Standard 1 to 4

Building rock-solid literacy, numeracy, and disciplined study habits through structured Maharashtra State Board foundational learning.

Foundational Scholastic Stage

Developing conceptual clarity and joyful discipline.

In Standards 1 through 4, students transition from early activity-based exploration into formal academic disciplines. Our primary curriculum focuses on building deep fluency in English reading, writing, and articulation, along with foundational Marathi and Hindi.

Mathematical operations are grounded in logical reasoning rather than rote memory. Environmental Studies introduces young learners to the wonders of nature, family community, health, hygiene, and civic values.

Grades Offered Std 1, 2, 3 & 4
Affiliation Maharashtra State Board
Medium English Medium
