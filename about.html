Heritage & Educational Mission

Dedicated to quality education and holistic character since 1944.

Dnyandeep English Medium School & Jr. College is an established educational institution in Manjari Khurd, Pune, committed to building academic excellence and moral values in every child.

Our History

Over eight decades of service to our students and community.

Established since 1944, Dnyandeep English Medium School provides education from the primary level through secondary school (Junior College) with a strong reputation for academic excellence and student discipline. The school features well-equipped facilities including science and computer laboratories, a comprehensive library, and sports grounds.

Dnyandeep School is committed to providing quality education that builds strong academic foundations, moral values, and life skills in students. With a team of experienced and dedicated teachers, the school focuses on the holistic development of every child, encouraging excellence not only in academics but also in sports, arts, and extracurricular activities.

Our students are mentored to develop intellectual curiosity, emotional maturity, civic responsibility, and resilience to navigate future academic and professional endeavors.

Guiding Principles

Vision, Mission & Core Values

The foundational ethos that shapes our classroom teaching, administrative integrity, and student care.

Vision

Our Vision

To be a premier educational institution in eastern Pune that empowers every child with foundational literacy, scientific inquiry, ethical leadership, and self-discipline to contribute meaningfully to society.

Mission

Our Mission

To deliver structured Maharashtra SSC Board education combined with affordable fees, modern labs, physical education, and individual attention, ensuring all students achieve their fullest scholastic potential.

Ethos

Our Core Values

Integrity, respect, discipline, curiosity, and inclusivity. We instill in our students a spirit of mutual support, cultural appreciation, and community responsibility.
