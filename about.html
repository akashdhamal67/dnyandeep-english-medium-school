About Dnyandeep English School

Nurturing young minds through quality education, strong values, and holistic development in Manjari Khurd, Pune.

Established Since 1944

A good education is a foundation for a better future

Established since 1944, it provides education from the primary level through higher secondary (Junior College) with a structured curriculum and supportive teaching environment.

Dnyandeep School is committed to providing quality education that builds strong academic foundations, moral values, and life skills. Our school focuses on holistic development of students through academics, sports, and cultural activities.

Leadership Message

Principal's Message

Welcome to Dnyandeep English School. Since 1944, our core endeavor has been to create an encouraging, disciplined, and nurturing environment where every child discovers their inner potential and develops high moral values.

"We believe that education is an important aspect in every person's life. It plays a vital role in shaping character, building knowledge, and developing skills that prepare individuals for future challenges."

Our Vision

To be a premier educational institution in eastern Pune that empowers every child with foundational literacy, scientific temperament, and ethical fortitude.

Our Mission

To deliver structured Maharashtra SSC Board education combined with affordable fees, modern labs, physical education, and personal mentorship for all students.

Our Core Values

Integrity, respect, discipline, curiosity, and inclusivity. We instill in our students a spirit of mutual support and community responsibility.
