Heritage & Mission

Over eight decades of service to our students and community.

Founded in 1944 in Manjari Khurd, Pune, Dnyandeep English Medium School & Jr. College stands as a respected pillar of structured education and moral character.

The Dnyandeep Legacy

The Sacred Flame of Learning and Holistic Character

Established since 1944, Dnyandeep English Medium School provides education from the primary level through secondary school (Junior College) with a strong reputation for academic excellence and student discipline. The school features well-equipped facilities including science and computer laboratories, a comprehensive library, and expansive sports grounds.

The school's emblem — the Mashal (sacred torch) — represents the light of true education: burning through the darkness of ignorance, instilling wisdom, and guiding future generations of Indian citizens toward righteous living and intellectual excellence.

Dnyandeep School is committed to providing quality education that builds strong academic foundations, moral values, and life skills in students. With a team of experienced and dedicated teachers, the school focuses on the holistic development of every child, encouraging excellence not only in academics but also in sports, arts, and extracurricular activities.

Guiding Principles

Vision, Mission & Core Values

The foundational ethos that directs our classroom teaching, faculty mentorship, and student care.

Vision

Our Vision

To be a premier educational institution in eastern Pune that empowers every child with foundational literacy, scientific inquiry, ethical leadership, and self-discipline to contribute meaningfully to society.

Mission

Our Mission

To deliver structured Maharashtra SSC Board education combined with affordable fees, modern laboratories, physical training, and individual attention, ensuring all students achieve their fullest scholastic potential.

Values

Our Core Values

Integrity, respect, discipline, curiosity, and inclusivity. We instill in our students a spirit of mutual support, cultural appreciation, and community responsibility.
