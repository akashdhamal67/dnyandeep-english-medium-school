Academics / Secondary Section

Secondary Section: Standard 5 to 10 (SSC)

Academic excellence, rigorous scientific inquiry, and structured Maharashtra SSC Board preparation with consistent high pass records.

Maharashtra SSC Board Excellence

Discipline, mentorship, and systematic board preparation.

The Secondary Section at Dnyandeep English Medium School bridges academic discipline with critical thinking. Our faculty comprises experienced educators who understand the syllabus depth required by the Maharashtra State Board of Secondary and Higher Secondary Education.

From Std 5 onward, students engage in hands-on science experiments in dedicated laboratories, regular computer practicals, structured doubt-clearing sessions, and periodic mock examinations that cultivate calm confidence during board assessments.

Academic Methodology

How we prepare students for Std 10 SSC success

A time-tested pedagogical system combining continuous assessment, revision, and personal mentoring.

Methodology 01

Concept Mastery & Daily Practice

Every topic in Mathematics and Sciences is taught conceptually. Daily numerical practice and diagrams build deep understanding rather than superficial memorization.

Methodology 02

Science & Tech Exhibitions

Annual science exhibitions and working model showcases challenge students to apply physics, chemistry, and environmental principles to solve real-world problems.

Methodology 03

Mock Papers & Exam Strategy

Regular unit assessments, preliminary exams, and timed answer-writing practice familiarize students with Maharashtra SSC board paper patterns and time management.
