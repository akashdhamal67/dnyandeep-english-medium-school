Eligibility Guidelines

Age Criteria & Required Documents

Age Criteria (Pre-Primary & Nursery)

For Nursery admission, children aged 3 to 5 years are eligible as per Maharashtra State Education norms. Jr. KG and Sr. KG admissions require commensurate age completion by the commencement of the academic session.

Required Documents for Admission

  • Official Birth Certificate (original & copy)
  • Previous School Leaving Certificate (Std 2+)
  • Previous Class Report Card / Marksheet
  • Student & Parent Aadhaar Card copies
  • Recent Passport-size Photographs
  • Address Proof (Electricity bill / Ration card)

Affordable & Transparent Fees

Dnyandeep School adheres to a strict policy of transparent and affordable fees without hidden levies, ensuring high standard education is accessible to all families in and around Manjari Khurd.
