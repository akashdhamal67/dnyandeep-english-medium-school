Admissions Guidelines & Eligibility

Clear, structured, and transparent admissions from Nursery through Standard 10 (Secondary SSC) with an affordable fee structure and no hidden costs.

Procedure

The Three-Step Admission Journey

We ensure an accessible and respectful interaction process for parents and students.

01

Online Application

Complete the official online registration form with student demographic details, parent contact information, and target grade level.

02

Document Verification

Visit our administrative office in Manjari Khurd for document verification. Meet with academic coordinators for an informal student interaction.

03

Enrollment & Welcome

Pay the transparent term fees, collect the official fee receipt, student handbook, and academic calendar before the commencement of the term.

Norms & Guidelines

Age Eligibility Norms (2026–27)

Grade / Section Age Benchmark Class Focus
Nursery 3 Years completed Sensory discovery & play
Junior KG 4 Years completed Phonics & motor coordination
Senior KG 5 Years completed Early reading & numeracy
Std 1 (Primary) 6 Years completed Structured Maharashtra State Board
Std 2 to Std 10 As per TC from recognized school Maharashtra SSC syllabus

* Note: Age criteria are determined in accordance with Maharashtra State Department of Education guidelines.

Checklist

Documents Required

  • Original Birth Certificate (for Nursery to Std 1)
  • Original School Leaving Certificate / Transfer Certificate (Std 2 and above)
  • Previous Academic Year Marksheet / Progress Card
  • Student & Parents Aadhaar Card photocopies
  • Recent passport-size photographs (3 copies)
