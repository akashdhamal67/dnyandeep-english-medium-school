Eligibility Guidelines

Age Criteria & Norms

Dnyandeep English Medium School strictly adheres to the age guidelines mandated by the Maharashtra State Education Department:

Nursery: 3 to 5 Years
Junior KG: 4 to 5 Years
Senior KG: 5 to 6 Years
Class 1st: 6 Years Complete

For lateral entry into Class 2nd through 10th (SSC), admissions are granted based on seat availability and evaluation of previous school records.

Checklist

Required Documents

Birth Certificate Original certificate issued by Municipal Corporation or Gram Panchayat (mandatory for Nursery & Std 1).
School Leaving Certificate (LC) Original countersigned LC / Transfer Certificate from the previous recognized school (for Std 2 onwards).
Identity & Address Proof Photocopies of student’s and parents’ Aadhaar cards and residential address verification.
Photographs & Academic Records Recent passport-size color photographs of the student and progress report from the previous grade.
Fee Policy

Affordable & Transparent Fee Structure

Dnyandeep School adheres to a strict policy of transparent and affordable fees without hidden levies, ensuring that quality Maharashtra SSC Board education remains accessible to all families in our community. Fee schedules are provided openly during campus visits and online admissions.
