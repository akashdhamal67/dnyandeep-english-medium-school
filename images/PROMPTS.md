# Dnyandeep English School - Photorealistic AI Image Prompts

This document contains the prompt specifications, target filenames, aspect ratios, and dimensions for the **15 photorealistic documentary-style photographs** requested to replace all illustrated assets on the website.

---

## Global Photography Style Directives
- **Prefix for every prompt:**
  `Photorealistic candid photograph, shot on a full-frame camera, 35mm lens, natural daylight, documentary style, real people, realistic skin and fabric textures, authentic Indian school in Maharashtra.`
- **Suffix for every prompt:**
  `Not an illustration, not a painting, not 3D, not cartoon, no text or logos.`
- **Uniform details:** Students wear a green-and-white plaid uniform (cream blouse/shirt, dark green trousers/skirt/pinafore, dark green socks).
- **Faculty:** Modest, dignified Indian formal clothing (e.g. elegant cotton/silk-blend sari in green, cream, or earthy tones).
- **Format Requirements:** Save as optimised WebP (quality ~80, under 150 KB each), plus a 640px-wide version (`-640.webp`).
