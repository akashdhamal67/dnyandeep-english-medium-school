# Photorealistic AI Image Prompts for Dnyandeep English School

This document defines the complete prompt specification and generation instructions for **photorealistic documentary-style AI photography** to replace all illustrated slots across the Dnyandeep English School admission landing page.

---

## 1. Visual Style & Photographic Direction
- **Camera & Optics:** Natural documentary photography shot on full-frame cameras with a 35mm or 50mm prime lens, shallow depth of field (`f/2.0` - `f/2.8`), soft natural daylight, warm true-to-life colors, subtle filmic texture/grain.
- **Atmosphere & Setting:** Authentic, genuine Indian school campus in Maharashtra (Pune region). Unposed, candid, and natural.
- **School Uniforms:** Students dressed in school colors: forest green and white plaid patterns with cream/off-white shirts or blouses, dark green socks.
- **Faculty:** Teachers wearing modest, dignified Indian formal clothing (e.g. elegant cotton or silk-blend saris in green, cream, or earthy tones).
- **Quality & Anatomical Safeguards:**
  - Natural skin tones without airbrushed, plastic, or oversaturated treatments.
  - Natural hands with strictly 5 distinct fingers, realistic joint articulation.
  - Natural facial proportions, eyes, and teeth.
  - Zero text, signs, logos, watermarks, or gibberish typography on boards or clothing.
  - No celebrities or identifiable real persons.
